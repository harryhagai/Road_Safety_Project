### 1.3.2 Specific Objectives

The specific objectives of this project are:

1. To develop a responsive web interface that enables citizens and commuters to report traffic violations easily without creating user accounts.
2. To integrate geospatial technology for accurately capturing, displaying, and analyzing the locations of reported traffic violations.
3. To develop a secure administrative portal that enables authorized road officers to log in, manage reports, review submitted evidence, and analyze road safety information.
4. To develop a report tracking and notification mechanism that allows anonymous reporters to check report progress using unique reference numbers.
5. To develop a road safety analysis mechanism for identifying high-risk locations and supporting preventive road safety measures.
